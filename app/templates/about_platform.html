Platform Capabilities

The Misinformation Detection & Evolution Tracking System leverages NLP, semantic similarity checks, and network graph models to audit information veracity.

✔️ Fact Verification

Connects to fact repositories (e.g. PolitiFact and Snopes via LIAR benchmark parameters) and evaluates statements to calculate truth probabilities.

📊 Sentiment Analysis

Evaluates document tone scores and categorizes emotional markers (anxiety, hostility, surprise) to identify emotional manipulation.

📈 Evolution Tracking

Traces structural mutation trees of claims showing how news stories adapted, exaggerated, or altered over time across media.

🧬 Narrative Mutation Detection

Monitors rewordings and linguistic updates used to spread distorted messages while bypassing platform blockers.

🕸️ Knowledge Graph Analysis

Extracts entity relations and maps semantic triplets to draw narrative relational maps showing how actors are interconnected.

🏛️ Source Reliability Assessment

Tracks publication history metrics, references integrity index datasets, and flags known biased networks.
